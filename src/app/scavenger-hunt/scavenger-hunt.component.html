How to Play

Join us on an exciting adventure through nature! Our scavenger hunt is designed to help you discover the beauty of the outdoors while having fun.

Rules

  • Take photos of each item you find
  • Be respectful of nature and wildlife
  • Share your discoveries with friends
  • Have fun and stay safe!

Get Started

Click the "Start Hunt" button above to begin your adventure! You'll be able to:

  • Create a custom scavenger hunt list
  • Track your progress
  • View beautiful nature photos
  • Learn interesting facts about nature
